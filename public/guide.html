DRAFT COACH — GUIDE

How to use the app + the full draft strategy (Bobby's draft guide, Parts 1 & 2)

🎮 How to use the app (30 seconds)

  1. Pick the game mode & map. The header dropdowns set the meta: Brawl Ball / Gem Grab / Hot Zone / Heist are AGGRESSIVE, Knockout / Bounty are PASSIVE. Every recommendation depends on this.
  2. Set the coin flip. Toggle who picks first in the draft: ⚔ Opponent picks first (you're penalized on picks) or ⭐ We pick first (you're favored). This changes the BAN GUIDE panel.
  3. Set the pick slot. Toggle Your pick 1 / Picks 2-3 / Picks 4-5 so the engine knows which rulebook to apply — first pick, pivot picks, and last-pick counter-picks all score differently.
  4. Fill the pick slots. Tap the 3 large slots per side (yours and enemy's) to cycle through taken drafts; or just tap brawler cards in the grid to fill the active zone. Locked enemy picks instantly produce counter suggestions.
  5. Read LOCK NOW. The top panel ranks the best available picks with reasons (class need, meta bonus, counter/danger tags). Portraits are color-coded by draft class.
  6. Follow the BAN GUIDE. It lists ban priorities for the current coin flip and mode meta, with tags explaining why each brawler is a ban (first-pick threat, 2/3 pivot, last-pick cheese).
💡 Workflow in ranked: ban phase → open BAN GUIDE (set coin flip) → first pick → set slot to 2-3 → watch enemy picks and re-check LOCK NOW → last pick → set slot to 4-5 and specifically counter the enemy composition.

⚔ Phase 1 — The coin flip decides everything

If WE pick first (you won the flip)

  • You get the draft's strongest pick — take it, but you must also draft “blind” against everything.
  • First pick (slot 1): in aggressive modes lock a meta anti-tank (e.g. Chester / Moe class picks). In passive modes (KO, Bounty) first pick is less punishing — range is fine.
  • Picks 2-3: pivot picks — Control or Space Makers to structure the comp. Don't over-commit to a second sniper.
  • Picks 4-5: this is where you are countered. Prefer flexible picks; a pure sniper early is a free loss.
Never waste your first pick on a one-trick counter pick — you're handing information away without pressure.

If the OPPONENT picks first

  • You see their pick before every one of yours — counter-picking is your edge.
  • Slot 1: still safe-bet: meta anti-tank in aggro. Don't hard-counter yet (they counter-back in 2-3).
  • Picks 4-5: your reward — last picks are near-uncontested. Grab direct counters to their locked comp.

🚫 Phase 2 — Bans (Part 2 rules)

Opponent picks first

Ban: the meta anti-tanks (aggro modes) or passive mainstays (KO/Bounty: utility snipers like Gus/Byron and Control like Lumi). Deny them the first-pick cheat they'd otherwise get.

We pick first

NEVER ban meta anti-tanks! If you ban them, you hand the opponent the best first pick for free. Instead ban: 2/3 pivots (Control/Space Makers) and last-pick cheese (meta throwers like Sirius/Larry/Berry/Juju/Ziggy, and Shade) so their picks 4-5 can't hide behind walls.
Ban logic recap: ban what THEY would pick, not what YOU fear fighting.

⚡ Aggressive vs Passive modes

AGGRESSIVE modes

Brawl Ball • Gem Grab • Hot Zone • Heist

  • Objectives compress fights → burst/stun dominates.
  • First pick: meta anti-tank.
  • Space makers (Nita, Moe, Chester...) as pivots.
  • Throwers risky mid-draft (they die to aggro divers).

PASSIVE modes

Knockout • Bounty

  • No respawn pressure → range wins map control.
  • Utility snipers (Gus, Byron) & Control shine.
  • Pure snipers (Piper, Brock...) as last pick — diving threat gets stronger as the round progresses.
  • Supports viable: Glowy/Kit heal/sustain out-values duels.

🤝 Phase 3 — Counter-picking inside the draft

  • Hard counter → strong pick: locking an enemy's counter is worth a big score bump; being countered by an enemy pick raises the warning.
  • Matchup memories: throwers die to mobility/dives; pure snipers lose to space makers; tanks die to throwers/snipers at range but feast on low-damage comps.
  • Mode-specific traps: e.g. Chuck is a Heist monster (mode-keyed counter) even when weak elsewhere.
  • Support rule: a Support (Gus, Poco, Kit...) must always pair with an independent damage pick — never as first pick of passive comps expecting to carry.
  • Current-meta bonus: Part 2's per-class top-5 meta picks get an extra score bonus — trust them when two options feel equal.

✅ Golden rules (TL;DR)

  • Draft classes, not counters to one brawler.
  • Won the flip: get value; lost the flip: get information.
  • Never ban meta anti-tanks when you pick first.
  • Ban cheese (throwers, Shade) when you pick first.
  • Pure snipers only when mobility threat is gone.
  • Support is a spice, never the meal.
  • Aggro mode → anti-tank first pick, every time.
